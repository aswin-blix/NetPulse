Test-method note:
The packaged input probe using raw Win32 mouse messages was unsuitable for Tk:
Tk reads GetKeyState for mouse-button transitions, which those messages do not
change. This was reproduced in a minimal independent Tk window and confirmed
against Tk's source. Input bindings were verified with Tk event_generate.
Packaged lifecycle checks were performed against the real executable.

Not physically exercised: unplugging hardware/VPNs, sleep/resume, Windows 10,
mixed-DPI multi-monitor setups, screen readers, or another user's computer.
Adapter changes, resets, long pauses and multi-monitor coordinates have
deterministic automated coverage. See BUILDING.txt for scope limitations.

Preview screenshots use sample traffic values. They are captures of the
working app, not performance measurements or promises of connection speed.
